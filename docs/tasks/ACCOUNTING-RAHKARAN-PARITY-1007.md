## Internal candidates that still need a contract decision

Existing Rubi data could support bounded implementations for chart-structure, journal-browser, journal-lines and a Rubi-defined ledger report without new financial mutation. Chart-structure output selectors are observed, but rendered result columns are not. Journal-browser has observed date/period/type/status filters and user/count columns for issued, changed and reviewed journals; complete historical aggregation semantics are unavailable for mutations that predate prospective state events. It therefore remains a distinct dependency rather than being flattened into the ordinary journal list. The exact population/output contract for the remaining report variants is incomplete, so this candidate does not relabel trial balance or turnover output as those source reports.

Detail-type conversion needs typed source/target detail semantics and collision rules. Opening deployment, nature adjustment, finalization and General Ledger voucher issuance need explicit generated-journal scope, period policy and replay invariants. Automatic execution needs its calculation/input contract. Subledger transfer needs a real destination contract. Debit/credit notices, aging, analysis, comprehensive and matrix reports need definitions for population and formulas. These are decision points, not implementation failures hidden behind generic success messages.

Tax thresholds, periods, identity, aggregations, returns, reconciliation, cancellations and external invoice operations require typed statutory schemas and approved external connectors. No credentials, acknowledgement, tax formula, filing result or dispatch success is fabricated.
