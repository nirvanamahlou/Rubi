# Automatic sandbox insurance

After final Sales confirmation, the durable Reservations inbox queues one policy per contract/passenger actually assigned INSURANCE. Selecting a checkbox in an unsaved form does not issue or charge anything. Existing policies remain nonautomatic by default and are not backfilled. Branch-specific insurer country/plan mappings and the existing API-only Travis sandbox environment are required.

The worker reads the latest eligible contract/passenger identity through a narrowly scoped internal Customer projection. National ID/passport and contacts are decrypted only for this purpose and audited; contact details may fall back to the contract customer. Missing identity/contact or mappings are shown on the affected policy instead of inventing data. Inputs are fingerprinted after submission. Requests use the persisted local policy UUID as provider contractNo. Create/Confirm phases are saved before calls; ambiguous outcomes are reconciled through Search and never blindly submitted again. Verified Active status, matching identity/contract/plan, actual policy number, premium and original PDF link are required before ISSUED. Confirmation and the owner-only notification commit together.

The owner explicitly selected the current sandbox. Status, filenames and screens identify these as sandbox policies, not real travel coverage. The provider handles its own credit accounting; issuance/download does not depend on the application's Finance document release. Existing ticket/voucher financial controls remain unchanged. Existing provider cancellation/refund and post-submission policy amendment are outside this flow; changed submitted inputs require insurer review. Current-intake downloads never attach a different contract version's policy.

Reservations and Sales each expose a protected summary plus an individual PDF endpoint. Sales applies existing SalesService.detail ownership/branch checks; the worker's document projection also checks Sales scope. Each policy download checks current contract/passenger membership and cancellation, fetches only a fixed HTTPS sandbox print URL without forwarding x-api-key, limits size and verifies the PDF signature. The UI offers per-passenger download and Download all in both workspaces; Download all requests separate PDFs rather than ZIP or re-rendered documents. Browsers may require their normal multiple-download permission; individual buttons remain available.

Official contract: https://sandboxapi.travis.ir/swagger/v1/swagger.json. A read-only lookup of a pre-existing synthetic SANDBOXTEST policy verified Search's Active/policyNo/payablePremium/plan.premiumCurrencyCode/pdfUrl fields and successful HTTPS PDF retrieval. No new live policy or real-customer provider request was executed during implementation. Automated tests mock provider writes, test ambiguous-outcome reconciliation, exact identity scope, no-Finance downloads and separate PDFs. The additive migration preserves old rows and requires a PDF receipt for automatically issued policies.

Rollout requires the Prisma migration before the new API, Web restart, and the existing server-only sandbox credentials. No key is added to Git or browser configuration. No production deployment is performed by the merge.
