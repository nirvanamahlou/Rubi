## Evidence and decisions

- Finance has registered events but no posted journal links. Purchase registration is not an approved, matched direct expense. Gross/net profit, balances and expense-derived indicators are withheld. No exchange-rate conversion or addition of different currencies.
- Ticket supply has real active allocations and expiring holds. Remaining capacity and weighted sell-through use their canonical definitions; snapshots never fabricate historical comparisons. A low-capacity warning needs an approved threshold.
- Sales cancellation uses status-change time; stay nights use actual check-in/out. Contract/hotel counts deduplicate repeated stays while stay averages retain each independent stay. Contract amount is not fabricated as independent hotel/service revenue.
- Customer signals combine Finance-confirmed purchases and structured leads via owner-public contracts, canonical Customers identity and pair/category deduplication. Refunds lacking service allocation withhold incomplete behavior totals. Free text is never classified as interest. Interest coverage is a count per its approved definition, not a percentage.
- Attendance uses the latest record per employee/day and accepted normalized minutes. Pending/absent/nonworking records, unapproved payroll drafts and absent appraisals do not imply workdays/performance.
- Call metrics reuse the protected native Call Center report and its answer attribution; no actor privilege is added. Unsupported manual-call attribution withholds incomplete totals. General Support excludes internal workbench and Call Center requests.
- Documents enforce owner-domain, confidentiality, deletion and branch scope. Issuance uses actual Reservation passenger issue documents and eligible noncancelled Sales contracts, not general files or allocations.
- Grouping uses opaque internal reference keys, preserving distinct same-name employees/cities/hotels/units/channels. Raw references and grouping keys never enter chart payloads. Monetary means are recomputed within each currency and safe numeric limits checked before float conversion.
