# Finance bank controls — 2026-11-17

The bank register and statement matching are under Accounting → Receipts and Payments, in bank definitions and bank account history. The Finance inbox contains payment controls and the broker ledger. No live bank connection, external balance inquiry, or exchange-office API is used.

- For a purchase of EUR 20,000, sending EUR 5,000 to the broker through an exchange office reduces the **office's available balance**, reserves the amount against the purchase, and leaves the amount payable at EUR 20,000. A linked broker receipt for that tranche reduces the payable to EUR 15,000. The next receipt for EUR 15,000 closes the purchase. A dispatch, a supplier assertion or a second submission of the same evidence is not a second payment. An already-funded purchase cannot be replaced by a new revision.
- The register groups purchases, confirmed payments, outstanding balances and funds in transit by broker and currency. The year-end cutoff includes unsettled earlier purchases. Contracts and journals link to existing source records subject to access. A source with no linked sales contract has no fabricated contract number. An immutable transfer ID distinguishes payments that share a contract number.
- Direct bank transfers require an approved external beneficiary account for the relevant owner, currency and company. Only a manager may designate an owner for a legacy source without a canonical counterparty. A suspended, blacklisted, receipt-only or altered account cannot authorize payment. This is an internal approval and identity match, not bank verification of an IBAN.
- Users import a statement CSV for a company-owned bank account and date range, supply statement balances and match its signed movements to posted ledger lines. A row can match only once; outstanding differences prevent finalization. This version supports one-to-one matching, not automatic or split matches.
- The existing shared Sales cheque calculator applies down-payment and cheque terms across all three companies. No company-specific rates, schedules or overrides were introduced.
- Three additive migrations introduce registers, links, uniqueness and immutable purchase protections. Legacy documents and payments remain unchanged. They were exercised on a disposable PostgreSQL database with synthetic records; neither Rahkaran nor production was used for writes.

Verification includes real PostgreSQL partial and full settlement, retry protection, purchase replacement rejection and statement reconciliation; direct bank beneficiary eligibility tests; CSV parsing and shared cheque-plan tests.
