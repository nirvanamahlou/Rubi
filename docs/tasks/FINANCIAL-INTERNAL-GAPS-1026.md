## Repairs

- Petty-cash funding, top-up and return use an explicit ACCOUNT_ONLY payment
  source policy for every currency. The active account must match the request
  branch and currency; suppliers and conflicting sources are rejected. Other
  foreign supplier-payment routes retain their prior behavior.
- Every operational request approval requires an actor other than its requester,
  extending the existing petty-cash invariant. Review/payment permissions,
  statuses and buttons are unchanged. This is a locally inferred internal
  control, not a legal rule or an amount-based approval matrix. It does not
  require three different people and does not retroactively invalidate approvals.
- New CHECK requests require the existing reference field even without a linked
  partner organization. This closes an inconsistent validation path; it does not
  create a full check lifecycle or external registration.

## Remaining boundaries

- Check deposit/endorsement/bounce, transfers between actual accounts and loan
  repayment schedules need source/destination or lifecycle commands not present
  in the current forms. No hidden automatic operations or fabricated defaults.
- HR referral responses remain responses: an ANSWERED status is not a payment
  authorization. Loan currency/installment allocation and approved claim-payment
  linkage must be explicit before a complete bridge can be implemented.
- An amount-based multi-stage matrix needs configured thresholds, currencies and
  approvers. No guessed thresholds or reuse of unrelated accounting policy.
- Legal payroll calculations depend on approved statutory configuration; current
  workflow continues to record actual gross/deduction/net values. No rates inferred.
- External bank, tax authority, Sayad and provider integrations remain outside scope.
