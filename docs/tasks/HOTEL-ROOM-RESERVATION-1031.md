# Per-hotel reservation forms and room-based purchase proposals

COMPUTER_ID=PC-A. User authorizes implementation and develop merge. Confirmed decisions: each room is Hotel P.P × a dimensionless Ratio × that hotel's actual nights, with no additional guest multiplier; all rooms of one hotel share one currency. Every hotel has its own broker, versioned supplier form and independently priced/count-based transfer, including explicit zero. Supplier-form prices only prefill an editable purchase draft. Only an explicit purchase registration records costs.

## Implementation and boundaries

- Canonical hotel stays are projected from the approved Sales snapshot. Additional hotel keys are scoped to their intake, itinerary position and hotel UUID. Existing primary hotel and transfer coverage keys remain compatible with older purchase revisions. Room types/capacities and booked child bands seed execution choices; saved choices remain intact and missing facts are not fabricated.
- Existing immutable workflow revisions store independent hotelSupplierForms entries and sent snapshots, with active canonical broker validation. Form edits do not change Sales prices, allocations, voucher or financial release. Old single-form responses remain readable. Current-output rebasing updates each stay separately; changed canonical stay details invalidate its stale room quote. History rows remain unchanged.
- Each hotel gets its own authenticated PDF/share scope and room-price table. The table includes room names, P.P, ratio, nights and proposed totals, with an independent transfer/count row. The standard four header cells and NOTICE numbering remain intact. Invalid/foreign hotel keys fail after source authorization.
- The buyer loads proposal values, can revise them without a write, and explicitly registers one atomic hotel/transfer purchase batch. The server recomputes every room total and checks the exact room ordinals, positive ratio, nonnegative amounts, active currency, transfer counts/capacity, canonical stay and independent supplier. Dates/room composition come from the saved execution form, with a supplier-form version check before and inside the purchase transaction. The existing purchase CAS, idempotency, FX evidence, approval and payment flows are retained. The recorded room calculation includes immutable names, nights and per-room totals.
- The purchaseSnapshot additive projection publishes only the server-owned execution snapshot captured by the form. Registered hotel and transfer costs sum per currency in the Sales profit projection; older primary/covered transfer revisions are superseded through existing keys and the legacy hotel fallback is never added twice. A proposal alone never becomes a cost, payable, financial approval or sale-price adjustment.
